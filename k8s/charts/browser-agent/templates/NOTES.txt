ISTIO INGRESS
-------------
The Gateway service is exposed via Istio IngressGateway.
To get the Minikube Ingress IP and port:

  export INGRESS_HOST=$(minikube ip)
  export INGRESS_PORT=$(kubectl get svc istio-ingressgateway \
    -n istio-system -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[?(@.name=="http2")].nodePort}')
  echo "http://${INGRESS_HOST}:${INGRESS_PORT}"
